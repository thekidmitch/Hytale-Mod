# Features

This document distinguishes demonstrated functionality from future plans.

## Demonstrated or Implemented in the Prototype

- Java-based Hytale plugin.
- Local Ollama conversation integration.
- Erik companion personality prompt.
- Natural responses to ordinary player chat.
- Persistent memory and conversation context.
- Player preference tracking.
- Relationship information.
- Independent goal records.
- Journal and reflection records.
- Custom Erik NPC role definition.
- Successful Erik NPC spawning in a test world.

## Still Being Developed

- Reliable prevention of duplicate NPC spawning.
- NPC movement and navigation.
- More dependable autonomous behavior.
- Better integration between AI decisions and in-game actions.
- Persistent NPC state across world sessions.
- Multiplayer-aware conversation handling.

## Long-Term Goals

- Multiple individual AI NPCs.
- Distinct personalities, values, skills, and interests.
- Relationships between NPCs.
- Autonomous work and daily routines.
- Settlement construction and resource management.
- Trading, cooperation, conflict, and leadership.
- NPC decisions influenced by needs, goals, and relationships.
